Privacy (DRAFT: have a solicitor check before launch)

Mac app: speech is processed on your computer. Recordings, transcripts and notes are saved on your computer only.

Web demo: your browser's speech service listens (Chrome and Edge send audio to the browser maker). Nothing is sent to RhemaFlow servers. Settings stay in your browser. Use "Try a sample sermon" to send no audio.

Early-access email: we keep your email and church name only to tell you about RhemaFlow. Ask us to delete it any time: hello@YOUR-DOMAIN.com.

Sermons can contain names and prayer requests. Look after saved transcripts and recordings.
