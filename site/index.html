Three steps. No production degree.

1

It listens

Speech is turned into text as the preacher talks. In the Mac app this happens on your own computer, even with the Wi-Fi off.

2

It finds the verse

“Turn to John chapter 3” or a half-remembered quotation. Clear references go up; unsure ones wait for you.

3

You approve, it shows

One tap or a spoken “next verse”. Blackout and Clear are always one button away.

Questions

Does it ever put a wrong verse up by itself?

Clear references can go up automatically. Quotations and anything unsure wait for a person. The operator stays responsible for what is shown.

Does my sermon audio leave my computer?

Mac app: no, it is processed locally. Web demo: your browser's speech service is used. Read the privacy page.

Does it work on Windows?

Not yet. The web demo works in Chrome and Edge on any computer.

Do we still need a song licence?

Yes. RhemaFlow ships no song words. UK churches need a CCLI licence for copyrighted songs.
